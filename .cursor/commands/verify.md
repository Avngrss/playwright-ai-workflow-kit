of what the gate cannot check and the independent one (light for an edited test, full for a new one — `/review` in
a new chat); a summary led by the verdict, its numbers copied from the verify block, and one next command.

one or two red tests → `/heal <spec>:<line> …` · three or more, or a CI run → `/triage` — a run summary's last line
names which.
